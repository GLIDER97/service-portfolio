The problem

Most apps lose users before they even install.

People decide in seconds. Screenshots and a text description rarely explain what your app does or why it's better. When nobody understands your app quickly, they scroll to the next one.

Your store listing doesn't explain the app.

Visitors can't see how it works, so they don't install.

Your ads have no strong video.

Google App Campaigns and social ads need videos in several formats. Without them, you're limiting your own campaigns.

Making a video yourself takes weeks.

Scripting, recording, editing, voiceover, music, exporting every size. Time you should spend on your app.

Why every app needs a video

The first thing visitors can watch is often left empty.

  1. 01

    The App Store and Google Play both let you add a video that appears at the top of your store listing. Many new apps leave it empty.

  2. 02

    Google App Campaigns can use your videos as ad creatives, and work best with videos in more than one orientation.

  3. 03

    Reels, Shorts and TikTok are vertical. A 20-second vertical promo is often the cheapest way to reach new users.

  4. 04

    A clear intro video helps new users understand the app faster, so more of them keep using it after installing.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do you need my source code?

No. I only need your app on the App Store or Google Play, a TestFlight or closed-testing invite, or an APK.

My app isn't public yet. Can you still make the video?

Yes. Add me as a tester (TestFlight on iOS, internal or closed testing on Android) or send an APK. Unreleased apps stay private, and I can sign an NDA.

Do you make videos for both iPhone and Android apps?

Yes. I make videos for iOS and Android apps, and one video works for both stores.

Can I use the video in Google Ads and Meta ads?

Yes. You own the final videos and can use them anywhere, including paid ads, your store listing and social media.

How do I add the video to my Google Play listing?

Upload the 16:9 version to YouTube and paste the link into the promo video field of your store listing in Play Console. I send short instructions with your delivery.

Is the music copyright-free?

Every track is made fresh for your video, so there's no stock music that someone else can claim.

What languages can the voiceover be in?

English, Hindi and many other languages. Ask me about yours.

How long will my video be?

Anywhere within your package's range: 15–30 seconds for a promo, 30–60 seconds for an intro. It isn't fixed at exactly 15, 30 or 60 seconds — the final length depends on the script you choose.

How many revisions do I get?

Two revision rounds per video. Extra rounds are $10.

What if my app needs a login?

Send me a demo/test account. Never share your personal account or passwords you use elsewhere.

What about my users' private data?

I record with demo data where possible and blur any personal information that appears on screen.

How fast is delivery really?

48 hours after you choose your script (Launch Bundle: 3–4 days). Need it faster? Add 24-hour rush delivery.

Do you make videos for games?

Casual and simple games, yes. For graphics-heavy 3D games, message me first.
